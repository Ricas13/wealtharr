# Fixed 60/40 annual-reference portfolio

**Status: model convention, not one uniquely authored strategy.**

The code's explicit variant is 60% `BROAD_EQUITY` and 40% `AGGREGATE_BONDS`; **fixed annual review, zero threshold**. Investors cannot change percentages, calendar or thresholds. A different variant requires a separate reviewed release.

Reference for the general asset-allocation concept: https://www.bogleheads.org/wiki/Asset_allocation , not a unique authored annual rebalance mandate.

£10,000 initial: £6,000 stocks/£4,000 bonds. £7,000/£3,000 at review: sell £1,000 stocks, buy £1,000 bonds. Final executable quantities depend on current validated prices, holdings, broker fees and wrapper eligibility. Human sign-off, dedicated golden tests, country-specific bond exposure definitions and production market data remain pending.
