# Built-in strategy source cards and regional fidelity

A strategy name is not a certified trading algorithm. Each card distinguishes verified source-derived weights from **Wealtharr's fixed review convention**, country-specific variants and outstanding evidence.

Allocation research checked 2026-10-09: HFEA's 55/45, Golden Butterfly's US 20%×5, Permanent Portfolio's 25%×4 and Buffett's 90/10. None has independent human strategy certification, commercial rights, verified market eligibility or completed production golden tests, so they remain research/draft.

**Country methods may vary structurally.** The Golden Butterfly author's Canada 40/20/20/20 differs from US 20/20/20/20/20. Such variants must be **separately versioned, code-reviewed immutable canonical releases**; don't merely substitute tickers and claim equivalence. Customers cannot edit the rules.

Publish only with original/attributable source, exact review convention and independently computed golden cases; verified exchange/ISIN, broker/wrapper eligibility, licensed market history/FX and independent sign-off. Research verification must never automatically enable a strategy.
