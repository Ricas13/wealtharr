# Global 80/20 (GBP) model

## Definition
This is a **Wealtharr-defined implementation** of a common 80% equity / 20% bond strategic allocation, not a claim that Bogleheads, Vanguard, or any single author prescribes these precise instruments, timing, or drift threshold. Canonical catalogue key: `80-20`; engine: `FIXED_ALLOCATION`.
- `GLOBAL_EQUITY_ALL_CAP_UNHEDGED`: 80%; developed and emerging world all-cap equity, no sterling hedge.
- `GLOBAL_AGGREGATE_BONDS_GBP_HEDGED`: 20%; global investment-grade aggregate fixed income, GBP hedged.
The percentages sum to 100%. No allocation to cash is required by the model. It is not suitable for all investors.

Sources for the broad allocation/rebalancing concepts: https://www.bogleheads.org/wiki/Asset_allocation and https://investor.vanguard.com/investor-resources-education/portfolio-management/rebalancing-your-portfolio . Both discuss strategic allocation and annual review; **neither specifies this exact Wealtharr model**.

## Rebalancing rule
`reviewFrequency: ANNUAL`, `rebalanceThreshold: 0.00`. At the annual review, calculate `total = equity + bonds + free strategy cash`; targets `equity = total * 0.80` and `bonds = total * 0.20`. Propose sales of overweight legs first, then purchases of underweight legs; invest cash in underweight legs. Rebalance after fills using observed prices; wait for a confirmed HOLD before closing the review. This annual zero-threshold convention belongs to Wealtharr and is *not* a universally specified rule. See `docs/strategy-engines.md` for whole-share/fractional rounding, fees, cash buffers and execution-residue behaviour.

## Instrument requirements
A regional listing must cover each exact economic exposure above, the customer's country and account wrapper, currency rules and all required purchase restrictions. A product merely described as "global stocks" or "bonds" is not enough. The eligible listing has to pass the existing `EXACT` market-mapping logic. No ticker is hardcoded. Absent or stale pricing causes a `DATA_REQUIRED` result.

## Owner-decision list
- The model deliberately fixes a globally diversified equity basket and GBP-hedged bonds; those are not author-prescribed components of an abstract 80/20 split.
- Keep the specific listed funds, domiciles and wrapper eligibility under the independently verified market-mapping gate.
- This is a GBP convention; do not automatically market the GBP-hedged bond allocation as a USD or EUR equivalent.
- Changes to weights, hedging or rebalance schedule require a separate version or named strategy, not silent changes to an immutable release.

## Golden tests
`tests/strategy-golden-80-20.test.ts` independently specifies allocation and rebalance arithmetic for exact target, equity overweight, bonds overweight, cash contributions and missing listings. The 80/20 weights produce GBP 8,000/2,000 for GBP 10,000, and with GBP 9,000/1,000 holdings require SELL GBP 1,000 equity and BUY GBP 1,000 bonds. The strategy engine and execution integration must also respect stale-data and insufficient-price safeguards.
